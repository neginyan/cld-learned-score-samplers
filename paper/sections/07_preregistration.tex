\section{Pre-registered predictions and their outcomes}
\label{sec:prereg}

Table~\ref{tab:prereg} lists every pre-registered prediction of the experiments reported here, in the order in which they were made.
Counting each metric of H1 and H2 separately, there are nineteen predictions: five were supported, one was supported only in the uninformative sense explained in Sec.~\ref{sec:mechanism} (H7), and thirteen failed.
Several failures changed the direction of the work: H3 located the damage before the last stretch, H5 showed that it is not made in a narrow window, and H6 showed that the exact-score amplification has the wrong order.
H9 showed that the learned score amplifies deviations in the last stretch in every metric, so that this stage alone does not explain the role of the metric.
